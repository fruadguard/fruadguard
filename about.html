About FraudGuard

FraudGuard is an AI-powered global anti-fraud ecosystem designed to help people identify suspicious digital activity, check potential scams and report fraudulent content.

Our Mission

FraudGuard aims to make digital spaces safer by combining fraud detection, community reporting and risk analysis in one platform. The goal is to help users make better decisions before trusting unknown links, phone numbers, email addresses and online accounts.

🌍

Our Vision

To build a trusted global platform that helps prevent digital fraud, protects users and strengthens digital trust.

🤝

Community Protection

Users can contribute reports about suspicious websites, phone numbers and email addresses, helping create a stronger fraud-awareness community.

Stay Safe Online

Never share passwords, OTP codes, banking credentials or recovery codes with unknown people. Be careful with unexpected payment requests, suspicious links and urgent messages. A clean FraudGuard result should not be treated as a guarantee that something is completely safe.
