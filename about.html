About FraudGuard

FraudGuard is an AI-powered fraud detection platform dedicated to protecting people from online scams, phishing attacks, fake websites, scam phone numbers, investment fraud, and other cyber threats.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make the internet safer by providing powerful AI tools that help people identify and report scams before they become victims.

Our Vision

To build the world's most trusted AI-powered anti-fraud platform, protecting millions of users across every country.
