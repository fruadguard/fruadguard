ABOUT INSPECT CALLER

About Inspect Caller

Inspect Caller is a Bangladesh-origin startup vision focused on building a global digital fraud intelligence and protection ecosystem.

OUR MISSION

Making the digital world safer.

Our mission is to help people identify suspicious digital activity, understand potential risks and report online fraud through a trusted platform.

Online scams are becoming more complex across websites, phone numbers, emails, social platforms, marketplaces and messaging services. Inspect Caller aims to bring detection, intelligence, reporting and user support together in one ecosystem.

OUR VISION

One trusted layer
for digital safety.

Our long-term vision is to create a global platform where users can access fraud intelligence, analyze suspicious information and report scams through responsible and authorized systems.

🔎

🧠

Intelligence

Organize available fraud information to help users understand potential risks.

Reporting

OUR APPROACH

Responsible by design.

Security technology must be useful without compromising privacy or creating unauthorized access to sensitive information.

🔒

Authorized Data

Platform integrations are intended to use public, user-provided or officially authorized data sources.

⚖️

Responsible Reporting

Reports are intended to support awareness, investigation and responsible response.

🛡️

User Protection
