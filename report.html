Community Reporting Guidelines

Please submit accurate and honest information to help protect everyone.

✅

Provide Accurate Details

Include correct phone numbers, website links or account information whenever possible.

📷

Share Evidence

If available, include screenshots or relevant details in your report description.

🚫

Avoid False Reports

Do not submit misleading or intentionally false information.

Reporting Tips

The more accurate your report is, the easier it is for FraudGuard to review and help protect other users.

  • Include the full website address if reporting a website.
  • Use the complete international phone number when reporting calls.
  • Describe what happened clearly and objectively.
  • Do not include passwords, OTPs or sensitive personal information.

🔒 Privacy Notice

Your report will be used to improve FraudGuard's fraud detection and community protection. Avoid including sensitive information such as passwords, PINs, OTPs or bank account credentials.
