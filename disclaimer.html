The website is intended to demonstrate the product vision, planned features, business model, roadmap and future technology direction.

Information presented on this website should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every described feature is currently available.

Inspect Caller is currently in the Idea / Pre-Launch stage.

Prototype Notice: Demo results must not be treated as verified real-world intelligence or official fraud determinations.

3. Fraud Detection Disclaimer

Inspect Caller is intended to assist users in identifying potential warning signs of fraud.

A caller, phone number, website, social media account, message or transaction marked as suspicious does not automatically mean that the person, organization or service is fraudulent.

Similarly, a low-risk or clean result does not guarantee that an entity is safe.

No automated result is a guarantee of safety or fraud. Users should independently verify important information before making financial or other sensitive decisions.

4. AI Disclaimer

Future Inspect Caller services may use artificial intelligence to analyze text, voice, images, links, messages and other information.

AI-generated results may contain errors, incomplete information or false positives and false negatives.

AI analysis should therefore be treated as an assistance tool rather than a definitive determination of fraud.

AI may be used for:

  • Scam pattern analysis
  • Suspicious message analysis
  • Potential phishing detection
  • Risk signal generation
  • Security awareness assistance

5. Financial Disclaimer

Inspect Caller does not provide financial, investment, banking or legal advice.

Users should not rely solely on an Inspect Caller result when deciding whether to send money, invest, borrow, purchase a product or enter into a financial transaction.

Never share passwords, PINs, OTPs, private keys or other sensitive authentication information with unknown individuals or services.
