Privacy Policy

Inspect Caller — Global Caller Security & Anti-Fraud Technology

Last Updated: August 10, 2026
Inspect Caller is currently an idea / pre-launch prototype. Some features described on this website may not yet be operational.

01 Introduction

Inspect Caller ("Inspect Caller", "we", "our", or "us") is a proposed digital safety and anti-fraud technology platform designed to help users identify, understand and report potentially suspicious digital activity.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when users access our website, applications and related services.

02 Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, Inspect Caller may collect information such as:

  • Account information such as name, email address and authentication data.
  • Phone numbers submitted for security or caller-checking purposes.
  • URLs, email addresses or other information voluntarily submitted for security analysis.
  • Fraud or scam reports submitted by users.
  • Device, browser and basic technical information required to operate and secure the service.
  • Usage information such as pages visited and features used.

03 How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and improve Inspect Caller services.
  • Process security checks and user-submitted reports.
  • Detect potential spam, phishing and fraud patterns.
  • Maintain account authentication and security.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and unauthorized access to our platform.
  • Develop future security, analytics and AI-assisted features.

04 User Reports

Users may voluntarily submit reports concerning suspicious phone numbers, websites, social media accounts, messages or other digital activity.

Important: A submitted report does not automatically establish that a person, phone number, account or organization is fraudulent. Reports may require verification before being treated as reliable security intelligence.

05 AI & Automated Analysis

Future versions of Inspect Caller may use AI-assisted systems to analyze security-related information such as text, images, voice signals and URLs.

Automated analysis may produce risk indicators or recommendations. Such results are intended as security assistance and should not be treated as definitive proof of fraud.

06 Firebase & Cloud Services

The planned platform may use services such as Firebase, AWS or other trusted cloud infrastructure for authentication, databases, storage and application functionality.

Where third-party infrastructure is used, information may be processed according to the applicable provider's terms and privacy practices.

07 Information Sharing

Inspect Caller does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide requested services.
  • Operate trusted technology and infrastructure providers.
  • Protect users and the platform against fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations or valid legal requests.

08 Security

We intend to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

09 Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, security operations, legal obligations and legitimate business requirements.

10 Your Choices

Depending on the final implementation and applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion.

Users may also choose not to submit optional information to the platform.

11 Children's Privacy

Inspect Caller is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

12 Third-Party Links

Inspect Caller may contain links to third-party websites, applications or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external services.

Users should review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing personal information.

13 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Inspect Caller develops new products, features, integrations or legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
