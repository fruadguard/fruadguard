How FraudGuard uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember settings and improve the user experience.

How We Use Cookies

FraudGuard may use cookies or similar technologies to keep users signed in, maintain security, remember preferences and understand basic website usage.

Some services used by FraudGuard, such as authentication or analytics providers, may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their policies.

Your Choices
