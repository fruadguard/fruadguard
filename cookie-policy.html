2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that can be stored on a user's device when visiting a website.

They can help websites remember information, maintain sessions, understand website usage and improve the user experience.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Type Purpose
Essential Required for basic website functionality, security and authentication.
Functional Helps remember preferences and improve the user experience.
Analytics May help understand traffic, performance and usage patterns.
Security May be used to detect suspicious activity and protect accounts and services.
Advertising May be introduced in future services where legally permitted and applicable.

4. Authentication & Session Cookies

If authentication is implemented, cookies or similar browser storage technologies may be used to maintain secure login sessions.

These technologies may help the platform recognize an authenticated session and protect account functionality.

Inspect Caller may use privacy-conscious analytics technologies in the future to understand website performance, traffic patterns and feature usage.

Analytics technologies will only be implemented according to applicable legal and privacy requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

Future versions of Inspect Caller may integrate with third-party services such as authentication providers, analytics providers, cloud infrastructure and security services.

These providers may use their own technologies subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

7. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow users to control or delete cookies through browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality, particularly authentication or other essential features.

Browser Controls

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block future cookies
  • Allow cookies only from selected websites

8. Local Storage & Similar Technologies

Inspect Caller may use browser technologies such as local storage for certain interface preferences, prototype functionality or session management.

Local storage is different from traditional cookies but can serve similar purposes in some applications.
