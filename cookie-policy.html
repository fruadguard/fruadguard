Cookie Policy

Learn how FraudGuard uses cookies to improve your experience.

1. Introduction

FraudGuard uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, security and user experience.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences and improve performance.

3. How We Use Cookies

FraudGuard may use cookies for website operation, user preferences, analytics, security improvements and service optimization.

4. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functions.

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand website usage and improve services.

Preference Cookies: Remember user settings and choices.

5. Third Party Cookies

Some trusted third-party services may use cookies for analytics, authentication or security purposes.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling some cookies may affect certain website features.

7. User Choices

Users can choose whether to allow certain cookie categories depending on their browser settings and available options.

8. Security & Privacy

Cookies used by FraudGuard are designed to improve security and user experience while respecting user privacy.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our services or technologies change. Any updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us
